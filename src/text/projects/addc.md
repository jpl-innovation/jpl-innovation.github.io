## What else is new

- **The first screen is the house itself**: scroll, and a 3D townhouse is surveyed, drawn, built floor by floor and entered, one short line of words at each stage. A loader tells the company's story in four photos while the page really loads, then opens onto it.
- **3D on every service page**: the architecture page shows the house as a line drawing, a clay model or in real materials, cut as a plan or a section, with the facade's colour chosen live; the construction page shows seven stages of a townhouse going up, from piling to handover; on the interiors page the visitor furnishes a room: three spaces, five floors, five wall colours, wood, fabric and light.
- **31 3D models** of the materials and furniture ADDC works with: concrete, steel, brick, stone, timber, tiles, a townhouse in seven stages and the rooms inside it.
- **Credentials in plain view**: ADDC's licence number, its grade and its expiry date, the equipment it owns, and its founding date and tax code.
- **Built to feel finished**: every picture opens large from its own spot, titles rise into place, photos wait in a dark frame instead of a pale card, and the 3D is prepared before it is seen, so scrolling stays smooth.

## How it was made

The content came from ADDC itself: 161 photos from its own sites and projects, its licence and equipment, its price tables and its site diary. Every page was checked at desktop and phone widths, every section scrolled through, and the frame rate measured on a 240 Hz screen before anything went live.
