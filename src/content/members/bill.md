I'm an academically driven student aspiring to become a mechanical engineer, with over six years of experience as a programmer in the Roblox game development space. Through game development, I've explored not just coding, but also video editing, graphic design, map building, digital art, storytelling, and technical writing; all contributing to creating immersive experiences.

Beyond game development, I serve as the programming lead on my high school's FIRST Robotics team, where I apply engineering principles to real-world systems. I'm particularly interested in aerospace engineering and complex mechanical systems. I also have a strong interest in geopolitics, having participated in over seven Model United Nations conferences.

What drives me most is systems thinking: understanding how components interact, where it breaks, and how it can be optimized. Whether I'm refining game mechanics, writing efficient code, or prototyping mechanical designs, I have a sharp eye for performance, precision, and impact.
